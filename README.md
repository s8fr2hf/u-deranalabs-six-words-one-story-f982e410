Write a complete story in exactly six words, on any subject. A judge model compares entries two at a time against the published brief, and the top three take the $5 pool.

## The story

> He planted trees he'd never shade.

Six words, one life: a man plants trees knowing he will never sit in their shade — the turn is that the planting is for someone else, for a future he will not see.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This copies `entry.txt` to `/output/entry.txt`.
